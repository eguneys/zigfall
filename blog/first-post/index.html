What is Project Zigfall?

First, a little context about me, I am Emre Güneyler. I have been participating in JS13k every year since 2019. My highest rated game is Coler become 63th out of 200 games. I have a keen interest in chess, and programming, such that I developed my own language for expressing tactical ideas in chess and published a research blog post How Queen’s Die, Analysing 6 Million Puzzle Database. That introduced me to Zig, and Typescript is my favorite language, I have been a fan of Zig.

Project Zigfall

I have started Project Zigfall, in order to make a 2D Platformer Game written in Zig, targeting Windows SDK, publishing on Steam, and keeping a weekly devlog and a blog about the process along the way.

I hope you learn something.

– Emre
